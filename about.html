About us

Built on thirty years of doing the work.

Rais Group Consulting exists for one reason: most business owners never get advice from someone who has actually run a business. We change that.

Philippa - Principal Consultant

Who we are

Rais Group Consulting is a small, independent advisory practice based in Medowie, NSW. We work with small and medium businesses across Australia - most of them owner-operated, most of them doing the hard yards without a sounding board.

Our principal consultant, Philippa, has spent thirty years working in and alongside businesses of every size: from the shop floor to the boardroom, and from the front counter to the back office. She has seen what actually works in Australian business, and what quietly kills it.

Now in semi-retirement, she took on consulting for one reason: too many owners are paying for advice from people who have never met a payroll, never stood in front of a disgruntled customer, and never lost sleep over a bad month. That is not advice. That is theory.

We do things differently. We listen first, we are direct, and we only recommend what we would do ourselves.

The experience behind the advice

Thirty years is not a number we put on a website. It is the time spent learning what works - and what does not.

The early years

Learning the job from the ground up - on the floor, behind the counter, dealing with customers, suppliers and staff when every decision mattered and there was no one to ask.

The middle years

Running operations, managing people, and watching close-up which businesses thrived, which survived, and which quietly fell over - and why.

The boardroom years

Sitting on the other side of the desk: ownership decisions, growth calls, difficult conversations, and the discipline of making numbers work when they do not want to.

Now

Bringing all of it to owners who need a straight answer and a practical plan - without the corporate theatre.
