About Rais Group Consulting

Philippa - Principal Consultant

Meet Philippa

Rais Group Consulting was founded by Philippa, who has spent thirty years working in and alongside businesses of every size - from the shop floor to the boardroom.

Now in retirement, Philippa wanted to put that experience to work for people who need it most: business owners who want straightforward, practical guidance from someone who has genuinely been there.

No theory. No corporate jargon. Just thirty years of knowing what works - and what doesn't - and the willingness to tell you honestly which is which.

Our approach

  • Listen first, advise second
  • Plain-English, practical recommendations
  • Advice scaled to your business and your budget
  • A straight answer, even when it is not what you want to hear
