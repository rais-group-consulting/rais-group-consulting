Common questions

Do you work with businesses outside NSW?

Yes. We work with clients Australia-wide. Most conversations happen over the phone or video call, and for local businesses in the Medowie / Port Stephens / Newcastle area we can meet in person.

How much does it cost?

It depends on what you need. A single advisory conversation is a fixed, simple fee. Longer engagements are scoped up front so you know the cost before you commit. The first conversation is always free.

Do you lock clients into contracts?

No. Every engagement is agreed up front, and you can stop any time. We would rather earn your business than trap you in it.

I am not sure what I need. Can I just talk it through?

That is exactly how most engagements start. Tell us what is on your mind, and we will help you work out whether - and how - we can help. If we are not the right fit, we will tell you that too.

What makes you different from other consultants?

We only give advice we would take ourselves, in language you do not need a dictionary for, and we have actually run businesses. Most advice sounds good in the meeting and falls apart in practice. Ours is built on what works when the rubber hits the road.
