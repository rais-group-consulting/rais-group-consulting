Our services

Help that fits how your business actually runs.

Every engagement starts with a conversation, not a contract. Here is what we do, who it is for, and what you can expect.

✎
Advisory

Business Advisory

Who it is for: owners facing a big decision - buying or selling, taking on a partner, expanding, restructuring, or just unsure which way is up.

What you get: a straight conversation about the numbers and the options, a clear recommendation, and an honest warning if something is a bad idea.

How it works: one session or a short engagement, depending on what the decision needs. You leave with a clear answer, not more questions.

⚑
Strategy

Planning & Strategy

Who it is for: businesses that have been running on instinct and want a plan they can actually follow.

What you get: a practical, written plan - where you are, where you need to be, and the steps to get there. The kind of plan that gets used, not filed away.

How it works: we work through it with you in plain English, then help you stay on track with regular check-ins.

⚙
Operations

Operational Guidance

Who it is for: businesses where the day-to-day is eating the owner alive - processes, people, pricing, suppliers, cash flow.

What you get: a clear look at where the friction is, and practical fixes you can implement without a corporate restructure.

How it works: we observe, ask questions, and give you a short list of what to change first - and what to leave alone.

★
Mentoring

Small Business Mentoring

Who it is for: owners going it alone who want a regular sounding board - someone who has seen it all before and has no agenda.

What you get: honest, experienced counsel on a regular basis. Someone to test ideas on, and to tell you when you are overcomplicating it.

How it works: regular sessions, as often as you need them. Many owners keep a monthly catch-up just to stay honest with themselves.

Common questions

How much does an engagement cost?

A single advisory conversation is a fixed, simple fee. Longer engagements are scoped up front so you know the cost before you commit. The first conversation is always free.

How quickly can we start?

Usually within a few days of your first call. Because we are a small practice, you talk to the person doing the work - not an account manager.

Will you work with a business our size?

If you have a business, you are the right size. We work with everything from one-person operations to established companies with staff and premises.

What if I only need one conversation?

That is fine. Many of our engagements are a single session - you get a straight answer, and that is all you pay for.

Not sure which service you need?

Tell us what is going on and we will point you in the right direction - even if that means pointing you elsewhere.
